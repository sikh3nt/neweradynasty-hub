# Dynasty Digital Hub

Create a premium, modern, futuristic personal brand website for Tozamile Sikhenjana, Founder and CEO of New Era Dynasty (2024/080819/07), a registered South African company based in Motherwell, Gqeberha, 6211, South Africa.

The website should have a luxury black, gold, silver, white, and subtle blue technology-inspired design with smooth animations, glassmorphism, responsive layouts, dark mode, excellent accessibility, and strong SEO optimization.

Do not use any personal photographs. Instead, use abstract visuals inspired by technology, entrepreneurship, business, networking, innovation, music, coding, and the township entrepreneurial spirit.

Testimonials & Reviews

Create a professional testimonial and review system where visitors and clients can submit their own feedback about my services.

The review form should allow visitors to enter:

Full Name

Company or Organization (optional)

Service Received

Star Rating (1–5)

Written Testimonial

Date

Optional profile photo or company logo

Include an administrator dashboard where I can:

Approve or reject reviews before they are published.

Edit reviews if needed (without changing the client's meaning).

Remove spam or inappropriate submissions.

Pin featured testimonials to the top of the page.

Display approved reviews in an elegant carousel and grid layout, with average star ratings and the total number of reviews.

Include a "Leave a Review" button on every service page so clients can easily provide feedback after working with me.

Include anti-spam protection, email verification or CAPTCHA, and mobile-friendly forms.

The overall experience should communicate professionalism, trust, innovation, resilience, entrepreneurship, leadership, creativity, and community impact while positioning Tozamile Sikhenjana and New Era Dynasty as a growing multidisciplinary technology and business brand with ambitions to make a meaningful impact in South Africa and beyond. Client Portal

Build a secure, professional Client Portal that allows each client to access their own personalized dashboard after hiring my services.

Secure Login

Email and password authentication.

Optional two-factor authentication (2FA).

Password reset functionality.

Role-based access (Client and Administrator).

Client Dashboard

After logging in, each client should be able to:

View all active and completed projects.

Track project progress with milestones and estimated completion dates.

Receive project updates and notifications.

View tasks completed and upcoming work.

Communicate with me through a secure messaging system.

Upload files, images, documents, and project requirements.

Download completed work, deliverables, invoices, quotations, contracts, and receipts.

View payment history and outstanding balances.

Request support or maintenance for completed projects.

Book follow-up consultations or meetings.

Submit feature requests or project revisions.

Verified Reviews & Testimonials

Once a project has been marked as completed, automatically invite the client to leave a Verified Review.

The review form should include:

Full Name

Company or Organization (optional)

Service Received

Project Title

Overall Rating (1–5 stars)

Written Testimonial

Date of Submission

Optional Profile Photo or Company Logo

Each approved review should display a "Verified Client" badge, confirming that the testimonial comes from an actual completed project.

Administrator Dashboard

Create a comprehensive administrator panel where I can:

Create and manage client accounts.

Create new projects and assign them to clients.

Update project status and milestones.

Upload files and deliverables.

Generate quotations and invoices.

Record payments.

Send announcements and project updates.

Review, approve, edit (without changing the client's intended meaning), feature, or remove testimonials.

View website analytics, client activity, and review statistics.

Notifications

Notify clients by email or WhatsApp when:

A project is created.

A milestone is completed.

New files are available.

An invoice is issued.

A payment is received.

A support request is answered.

Their review has been approved and published.

Design

The Client Portal should match the premium branding of the main website, using a clean, modern, and responsive interface with a black, gold, white, and subtle blue color palette. It should feel secure, intuitive, and professional on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

The portal should reinforce trust, transparency, and long-term relationships by giving clients full visibility into their projects while encouraging authentic, verified testimonials that strengthen my professional reputation.
